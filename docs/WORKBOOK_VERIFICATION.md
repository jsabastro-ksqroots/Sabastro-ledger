## Other facts Phase 1–2 must design for

- **Negative debits exist.** 43 lines in Workbook A have a negative Debit (year-end reallocation entries 1642 · 2244 · 2260 · 2987), summing to −5,862.32. The app stores non-negative `debit_cents` / `credit_cents`, so the importer normalizes a negative debit of −3.00 into a credit of 3.00. Net totals per account and per class are unchanged, but the **gross** debit and credit totals each rise by 5,862.32: after normalisation Σ debit = Σ credit = **13,303,638.68** (the workbook's signed total is 13,291,914.04). Phase 2 must reconcile on net per account / class × sub-type and report both gross figures.
- **Four zero-amount single-line entries** (2024-07-12, transactions 2623–2626, `5212 Bank Fees` / `1101`, name "Bank of America", 0.00). They violate the ≥ 2 lines rule and the one-non-zero-amount rule. Decision P0-3 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`: import them as `VOIDED` transactions **with no lines** (account, name and memo kept in the header and a system note) with the reason "zero-amount placeholder in source workbook", so the 2,999 transaction count and provenance survive. Lines imported with amounts: 7,144 − 4 = 7,140.
- **One 100-line transaction** (#2987, 2024-12-31, the year-end reallocation across 9 expense accounts × properties) and several 16-line reallocation entries — the ledger UI must cope with wide journal entries.
- **Two exact duplicate line pairs** inside transactions (7,142 distinct `(txn, account, class, dr, cr)` of 7,144). Keep both; they are separate lines of one entry.
- `Checked?` column in Workbook B: 731 "Not checked", 102 "Checked - found it" — review artefact, ignore. 75 rows have user notes; 454 have ledger (system) notes.
- `Filled in by` has 32 distinct values; 357 rows start with "Jose" (→ `verified_by_owner = true`). 166 rows are blank.
- Venmo rows: 30 × `4101 Rental Income` (Shed), 5 × `5206`, 1 × `5208`, 2 × `2101` (security deposits out). Classes: The Shed 30, Sunnyside 3, Providence 3, Maloney 2.
- 25 expense-account rows carry a **positive** amount (refunds/returns) — the single-row → two-line rule handles them (Dr bank / Cr expense).
- "Questions for Jose" items 3–10 are open bookkeeping questions (Keystone tax bill, Comply startup cost, $5,000 PLA opening capital source, Eureka split detail, Southwest trip, three Amazon orders, Zelle vendor mismatch, Clubhouse lease) — they belong in the Phase 2 import report's "rows needing attention" list, not in code.
