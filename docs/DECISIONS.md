## Phase 1 decisions (2026-09-12)

| #     | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| P1-1  | **The lines on a bank-centric transaction's own bank account are derived, one per class**, on the opposite side of the account/class lines, and regenerated on every change. A split across two classes therefore has two bank lines. Users never edit those lines directly; a line on another own bank account (a transfer) is an ordinary account line (P1-18).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Each class balances on its own, which keeps the per-class "Bank" column of the workbook reconciliation meaningful and makes the bridge arithmetic uniform.                                             |
| P1-2  | **Bridge lines are exempt from the class attribution rule.** The receiver's `Cr 3101` line carries the class it received even when that class belongs to the other entity (a PLA client paying into the SREI bank gives SREI a `Cr 3101` with class Providence). The payer's `Dr 3102` line is class General. The per-entity balance trigger is what keeps bridge lines honest.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | DESIGN §3 already said "the class whose lines it received"; the first draft of the trigger rejected the money-in case.                                                                                 |
| P1-3  | **Every change supersedes lines, drafts included.** The database still allows deleting lines under a draft, but the app has one code path: compute the full desired set of lines, keep identical ones, supersede the rest with the audit row id, insert the new ones.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | One tested path instead of two; history of drafts costs a few rows.                                                                                                                                    |
| P1-4  | **Every ledger write is one database transaction** (`db.$transaction`). The balance rules are deferred constraint triggers that fire at commit, and the lock override is a transaction-local `set_config`. Server actions never write outside a transaction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | A half-written entry can never be committed; the override cannot leak into other writes.                                                                                                               |
| P1-5  | **Lock override bookkeeping**: the guard runs before any write; with no reason it throws `LockedYearError` and the action returns `needsOverride` (years + states) so the UI shows the full-screen warning and retries with the reason. With a reason: `set_config`, one `tax_year.override` audit row per locked year (`is_lock_override = true`, reason, subject), `override_count + 1`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | CLAUDE.md wants the warning, the typed reason, the audit override record and the badge; counting per year lets each year's badge be exact.                                                             |
| P1-6  | `transactions.seq` is a running number shown as `#123` in the ledger and audit labels. Imported rows keep the workbook's numbers in `source_ref`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Humans need a short handle; UUIDs are not it.                                                                                                                                                          |
| P1-7  | **One user note per transaction** (partial unique index), edited in place with an audit row, never deleted (an empty note is kept as an empty row). System notes are unlimited and append-only (trigger). Notes are not covered by the year lock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Matches the workbook's single "My notes / answer" column; notes never change numbers.                                                                                                                  |
| P1-8  | **Permissions**: "Review and confirm" creates drafts, posts, edits drafts, writes user notes and may "Save and post" a hand-entered row directly; "Edit posted transactions" is needed to edit, split, unsplit or void a posted row; "Close and file tax years" closes/files and ticks the checklist; only the Owner re-opens. All checked on the server in every action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | CLAUDE.md's Limited checklist maps cleanly; posting directly keeps Jamin's daily entry fast (question 4 in PROGRESS.md asks whether to force the queue).                                               |
| P1-9  | **`@tanstack/react-table` pinned at 8.21.3.** Version 9 shipped in August 2026 with a reworked API; v8 is stable and well documented.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | "Fewer dependencies, pinned versions"; no reason to adopt a fresh major during Phase 1.                                                                                                                |
| P1-10 | The ledger loads one entity-year at a time (every transaction with a live line attributed to the entity, or homed there) and filters, sorts and pages in the browser; page size 100 by default.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Simplest fast grid for a few thousand rows a year; virtualisation is a later option (see PROGRESS).                                                                                                    |
| P1-11 | **A journal entry must balance overall before bridging** (Σ debits = Σ credits of the user's lines). Only per-entity imbalances are bridged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Otherwise the bridge would silently "fix" a typo by inventing a contribution.                                                                                                                          |
| P1-12 | **Splits**: parts by amount (must add up exactly) or by percentage (must total 100.00 %; cents floored, remainder to the largest share). Children point at the superseded original (`parent_line_id`) and the database checks they add up to it; **unsplit** inserts a single line that also points at the original, so history is a chain. An inline edit keeps the edited line's parent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | CLAUDE.md: "must sum to the original"; the chain lets Phase 5 re-split model-linked lines.                                                                                                             |
| P1-13 | Voiding a draft in a closed year goes through the lock guard like a posted row (the trigger fires on the move to `VOIDED`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Consistency with the database rule; rare in practice.                                                                                                                                                  |
| P1-14 | Editing a journal entry replaces all its lines (parent links reset); editing the amount/account/class of a split bank row is refused until it is unsplit or edited line by line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Keeps the split-children invariant simple.                                                                                                                                                             |
| P1-15 | **Money on the wire**: bigint cents in the database layer; integer `number` cents in what server components pass to the browser (safe far beyond any real total); formatting at the edge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | React cannot serialise bigint to client components.                                                                                                                                                    |
| P1-16 | Saved ledger views are private to the user who saved them (name unique per user); nothing is shared between users yet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Simplest useful version; a shared flag can come later.                                                                                                                                                 |
| P1-18 | **Only the line on a bank-centric transaction's OWN bank account is derived.** A line on another bank account's ledger account is the user's account line (a transfer between own accounts): it is editable, splittable, and the row's amount is what moved through the row's own bank account. The account picker lists bank accounts in their own group at the bottom (labelled "only for a transfer" in the simple-row editor) and hides the ledger accounts of closed bank accounts unless a row already points at one; the server likewise refuses a closed bank account as a new counterpart on a row dated after its closing date. A journal entry that touches exactly one bank account shows that account's movement as its amount. | Found by Jamin on 2026-09-13: a "transfer" entered with 1102 as the account showed $0.00 and could not be edited ("split into 0 lines"), and the closed 1102 account was the first choice in the list. |
| P1-17 | The year-end checklist has five items; two are computed live (no drafts/flagged dated in the year; at least one posted adjusting entry), three are stubs until Phases 4/5/6 that can be ticked by hand or overridden with a reason. "Mark closed" is enabled only when every item is satisfied.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | CLAUDE.md: "items can be stubbed, the gate must work".                                                                                                                                                 |
